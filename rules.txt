# Super 4 - Game Rules (Draft)

## Objective

The objective of the game is to finish with the **lowest total card value** among all players.

At any point during **your own turn**, if you believe your total is the lowest, you may declare **"Stop."**

Once Stop is declared:

* Every remaining player gets **one final turn** until play comes back to the player who declared Stop.
* After that, all cards are revealed.
* If the player who declared Stop has the **lowest total**, they win.
* Otherwise, they lose.

---

# Game Setup

* Each player is dealt **4 face-down cards**.
* Each card position is fixed and labeled **1, 2, 3, and 4**.
* The remaining cards form the **draw deck**.

Before the game officially begins:

* Every player is allowed to look at **only their first two cards**.
* Players must memorize:

  * the value of each revealed card.
  * the position (1–4) where each card is located.

After this initial preview:

* All cards are turned face down.
* Players may never freely look at their own cards again unless a card power allows it.

Memory is one of the core mechanics of the game.

---

# Normal Turn

During a player's turn:

1. Draw the top card from the deck.
2. The drawn card is visible to everyone.
3. The player now has two choices:

   * Keep the card.
   * Discard the card to the table.

---

# Matching Your Own Card

If the player draws a card and believes they already have the same value somewhere among their four hidden cards, they may attempt a match.

Process:

1. Draw a card.
2. Click one of your four hidden cards.
3. If the selected hidden card has the **same value** as the drawn card:

   * Both cards are discarded.
   * The player now has one fewer card.
4. If the selected card does **not** match:

   * The selected card is revealed to everyone.
   * It is placed back in the same position.
   * The drawn card is returned/discarded as per game rules.
   * The player receives **one penalty card** from the deck.

Since players cannot normally see their own cards, successful matching depends entirely on memory.

---

# Matching a Card Discarded by Another Player

Whenever any player discards a card onto the table, every other player may immediately try to match it.

Only the **first player** who reacts successfully gets the opportunity. Others lose that chance.

There are two possibilities.

### 1. Matching your own card

A player may believe they have the same card hidden in their own hand.

If correct:

* The matching card is discarded.

If incorrect:

* The chosen card is revealed to everyone.
* It is returned to its original position.
* The player receives one penalty card.

---

### 2. Matching another player's card

If a player remembers that another player has the discarded card at a particular position, they may attempt to remove it.

Process:

1. Select the target player's card position.
2. If correct:

   * That matching card is removed.
   * The acting player gives one card from their own hand to replace the removed card.
3. If incorrect:

   * The targeted card is revealed to everyone.
   * It is returned to its original position.
   * The acting player receives one penalty card.

This makes remembering both your own cards and your opponents' cards extremely important.

---

# Card Powers

Special powers are activated **only when a drawn card is immediately discarded to the table.**

If the player keeps the drawn card, **its power is lost**.

---

## Card 7 or 8 — Look at Your Own Card

After discarding a drawn 7 or 8:

* The player may choose any one of their four hidden cards.
* Only that player gets to see its value.

No other player sees the card.

---

## Card 9 or 10 — Look at Another Player's Card

After discarding a drawn 9 or 10:

* Choose any player.
* Choose one of their hidden cards.
* Only the acting player sees that card.

No one else gains any information.

---

## Card 11 or 12 — Blind Swap

After discarding a drawn 11 or 12:

* Choose one of your hidden cards.
* Choose one hidden card belonging to another player.
* The two cards are swapped.

Neither card is revealed before or after the swap.

Everyone sees that a swap occurred, but nobody knows the card values.

---

## King (13) — Look and Swap

After discarding a King:

1. Choose one of your hidden cards.
2. Choose one hidden card belonging to another player.
3. Both selected cards are shown **only to you**.
4. You may then decide whether to:

   * Swap them, or
   * Leave them unchanged.

Other players can see that this action is taking place, but they never see the card values.

---

# Visibility Rules

Everyone can always see:

* whose turn it is,
* cards drawn from the deck,
* cards discarded,
* swap animations,
* power usage,
* successful or failed matching attempts.

Hidden information:

* Players cannot normally see their own cards.
* Players cannot normally see opponents' cards.
* Card values revealed through powers are visible **only to the player using that power**.

---

# Penalty Cards

A player receives one penalty card whenever they incorrectly attempt to match a card.

This applies to:

* incorrectly matching one of their own cards,
* incorrectly matching another player's card.

Penalty cards increase the player's total score, making it harder to win.

---

# Card Values

Most cards are worth their face value:

* Ace = 1
* 2–10 = face value
* Jack = 11
* Queen = 12
* King = 13

**Exception:**

The **Red King (King of Hearts)** has a value of **-1**, making it the most valuable card in the game.

---

# Core Strategy

The game is built around three main skills:

* Memorizing your own cards and their positions.
* Remembering cards revealed from opponents.
* Deciding the right moment to declare **Stop** before other players improve their hands.
