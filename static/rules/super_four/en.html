🎯 Goal

Finish with the lowest total of card values. On your turn you may call Stop if you think you're lowest — everyone else gets one final turn, then all cards are revealed. Call it right and you win; get beaten and you lose.

🃏 Setup

You get 4 face-down cards in fixed slots 1–4. At the start of each round you may memorise your own first two cards until the host starts play, for a maximum of 30 seconds. Then everything goes face-down and you must play from memory.

🔢 Card values

Ace = 1, number cards = face value, Jack = 11, Queen = 12, King = 13. Exception: both red Kings (Hearts and Diamonds) are worth −1 — the best cards in the game.

🔄 Your turn

  • Draw the top card (only you see it).
  • Keep it: tap a slot to swap it in; the new card stays hidden and the old card is discarded face-up.
  • Discard it: if it's a power card (7–13) its power triggers.
  • Match: tap a slot you believe already holds that same value — right and it's removed (fewer points!); wrong and you take a penalty card.

⚡ Table matches

Whenever a card lands face-up on the table, everyone has 4 seconds to match its rank, regardless of suit. The first correct match received by the server wins. You may throw multiple matching cards from your own or opponents' slots. For each opponent card you remove, choose one of your own cards to transfer into that exact slot. A wrong attempt returns all chosen cards to their slots and gives you a hidden penalty card; other players may still react before time expires.

✨ Card powers (only when you discard the drawn card)

  • 7 or 8 — look at one of your own cards.
  • 9 or 10 — look at one opponent's card.
  • 11 or 12 — blind-swap one of yours with an opponent's (no one sees them).
  • King (13) — look at one of yours and one opponent's, then optionally swap.

🏆 Scoring & winning

A game is 5 rounds and lower is better. Each round: the lowest hand wins (−3), everyone else loses (+1). Call Stop and win → −3; call Stop and get caught (not lowest) → a +3 penalty. Reach the exit score (10) and you're eliminated to a spectator. Lowest total after 5 rounds wins the game. (All values are host-configurable.)

🧠 Strategy

Remember your own cards and any you glimpse from opponents. Shed high cards, hunt the King of Hearts, and pick the perfect moment to call Stop.
