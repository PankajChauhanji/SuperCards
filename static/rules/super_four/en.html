🎯 Goal

Finish with the lowest total of card values. On your turn you may call Stop if you think you're lowest — everyone else gets one final turn, then all cards are revealed. Call it right and you win; get beaten and you lose.

🃏 Setup

You get 4 face-down cards in fixed slots 1–4. Before play you may look at only your first two — memorise them! After that everything is face-down.

🔢 Card values

Ace = 1, number cards = face value, Jack = 11, Queen = 12, King = 13. Exception: the King of Hearts is worth −1 — the best card in the game.

🔄 Your turn

  • Draw the top card (everyone sees it).
  • Keep it: tap a slot to swap it in; the old card is discarded.
  • Discard it: if it's a power card (7–13) its power triggers.
  • Match: tap a slot you believe already holds that same value — right and it's removed (fewer points!); wrong and you take a penalty card.

✨ Card powers (only when you discard the drawn card)

  • 7 or 8 — look at one of your own cards.
  • 9 or 10 — look at one opponent's card.
  • 11 or 12 — blind-swap one of yours with an opponent's (no one sees them).
  • King (13) — look at one of yours and one opponent's, then optionally swap.

🧠 Strategy

Remember your own cards and any you glimpse from opponents. Shed high cards, hunt the King of Hearts, and pick the perfect moment to call Stop.
