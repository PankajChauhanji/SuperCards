📱 Install Super Cards

Install the app on your phone to launch it like a native app from your home screen.

Android • Chrome

  1. Open the app in Chrome on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Tap Install and the app will appear on your home screen.

iPhone • Safari

  1. Open the app in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add and it will be available like a regular app.

Quick tip

If you do not see the option, make sure the site is opened in the correct browser and that your phone is using the latest version of the browser.
