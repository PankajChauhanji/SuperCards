Install the game to your phone so it opens from your home screen like a regular app, without typing the address every time.

Why install?

  • Open the game quickly from your phone home screen.
  • Keep it available like any other app.
  • Load faster after first use and stay ready for quick matches.

Android • Chrome / Edge / Samsung

  1. Open Super Cards in your phone browser.
  2. Tap the browser menu (three dots) in the top-right corner.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm by tapping Install or Add.
  5. You will see the Super Cards icon on your home screen.

iPhone • Safari

  1. Open Super Cards in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down until you find Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add and confirm.
  5. The game will appear on your home screen and launch like an app.

If the install option does not appear

  • Refresh the page and try again.
  • Use Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone for the best results.
  • On iPhone, the option appears only in Safari, not in other browsers.
  • If the app is already installed, you may see Open instead of install.

Need more help?

Tap the button again if the screen closes. The app is built to be easy to install and use from your home screen.
