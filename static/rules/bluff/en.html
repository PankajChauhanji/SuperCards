Bluff Rules

The primary goal is to be the first player to successfully discard all cards from your hand.

Finishing & Places

Shedding your last card does not end the game — it wins you a place. Play carries on for everyone still holding cards, so 1st, 2nd and 3rd are all played for.

  • Your last cards can still be challenged. You are only out once the next player has acted without calling Show — get caught and you pick the pile back up.
  • The game ends once 3 players have finished, or as soon as only one player is still holding cards. With 2 or 3 at the table that second rule ends it naturally.
  • Anyone still holding cards is ranked by how many cards they have left, fewest first. Card values do not matter — in Bluff a card is a card.

Core Gameplay Mechanics

  • One rank is locked for the entire round.
  • A round begins when a player sets a target rank and ends only when a challenge (Show) occurs, or when all players consecutively pass.

Playing Cards

Place 1 to 4 cards face down on the center pile and declare that they match the current target rank. You do not have to tell the truth.

Passing

If you do not have cards of the target rank, or if you do not want to risk bluffing, you can declare Pass. Your turn is skipped. If all players pass consecutively, the table is cleared.

The "Show" (Challenging)

If a player suspects that the person who just played lied, they can call "Show!". Only the exact cards that the active player just threw are flipped over.

  • Bluff caught: The bluffing player picks up the entire table pile. Challenger starts next round.
  • False accusation: The challenger picks up the entire table pile. Defender starts next round.
