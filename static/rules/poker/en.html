Poker (Texas Hold'em) Rules

Everyone starts with the same coins. Win pots by making the best five-card hand, or by betting until everyone else folds. When the game ends, whoever holds the most coins wins. Coins are play money only — they have no cash value.

Setup

  • 2 to 20 players, one 52-card deck, reshuffled every round.
  • The host picks the starting coins (default 1,000,000) and the number of rounds (default 10 rounds).
  • Blinds are set for you: the big blind is 1% of the starting coins and the small blind is half of that — 5,000 / 10,000 by default. The host can make them double every few rounds.
  • The host can switch on hand hints: each player then sees the name of the hand they hold, and its row lights up in the Hand rankings card. Off by default.

A round

  1. The two players left of the dealer button post the small and big blind.
  2. Everyone gets 2 private cards.
  3. Pre-flop betting, starting left of the big blind.
  4. Flop: 3 shared cards, then betting. Turn: a 4th card, then betting. River: a 5th card, then the last betting.
  5. Showdown: the best five cards out of your two plus the five shared cards wins.

Heads-up (2 players), the dealer posts the small blind and acts first before the flop.

Your options

  • Fold — give up the round. Coins you already bet stay in the pot.
  • Check — pass without betting, when nobody has bet.
  • Call — match the current bet.
  • Bet / Raise — any amount up to your whole stack (No-Limit). The minimum bet is one big blind; a raise must be at least as big as the last bet or raise.
  • All-in — bet everything you have. If you are all-in you can only win, from each player, as much as you put in; the rest goes into a side pot.

An all-in that is smaller than a full raise does not let players who already acted raise again.

Hand rankings (best first)

  1. Royal Flush — A K Q J 10, same suit
  2. Straight Flush — five in a row, same suit
  3. Four of a Kind
  4. Full House — three of one rank + a pair
  5. Flush — five of one suit
  6. Straight — five in a row (A-2-3-4-5 is the lowest, A-K-Q-J-10 the highest)
  7. Three of a Kind
  8. Two Pair
  9. One Pair
  10. High Card

Ties are decided by the remaining cards (kickers). Suits never break a tie; equal hands split the pot.

Between rounds

A round summary shows who won and every player's coins left. The host starts the next round, or it starts by itself after 30 seconds. Folded hands are never shown.

End of the game

  • The game ends after the last round, or as soon as one player has every coin.
  • Players are ranked by coins left; busted players rank below, the later bust higher. Medals go to the top three.

Timer

You have 30 seconds per turn. When time runs out you check if you can, otherwise you fold. Miss 3 turns in a row and you are sat out — still paying blinds — until you press I'm back.
